# Smart Ward Hub — Business Model Hypotheses

**Status:** Hypotheses only; replace with customer interviews and signed evidence.

## Buyer and user hypotheses

| Question | Current hypothesis | Evidence needed |
|---|---|---|
| Economic buyer | Hospital operations/quality/IT executive | Interview and budget owner confirmation |
| Daily user | Ward operator/nurse and supervisor | Workflow observation and usability review |
| Technical approver | Hospital IT/security/integration team | Architecture and security review |
| Clinical approver | Named clinical owner | Intended-use and pilot sign-off |
| Initial wedge | One ward with edge/offline monitoring and controlled handover | Design-partner problem statement |

## Value hypotheses

The product may reduce missed or delayed operational signals, improve handover traceability, reduce reconciliation effort during connectivity loss, and provide an auditable workflow boundary. These are not claims until a pilot defines baseline, measurement method, comparison period, sample size and decision owner.

## Pricing hypotheses

Test a site-based pilot fee, implementation/integration fee and recurring support/software fee. Keep hardware, integration, training, support, hosting and replacement costs separate so gross margin and deployment burden are visible. Do not set a final price from competitor websites alone; validate willingness-to-pay with the buyer and the cost of the current workflow.

## Customer discovery questions

Ask what event causes the buyer to spend, what the current system fails to show, who owns the risk, what evidence is required to approve a pilot, how long IT/security review takes, what integrations are mandatory, what a failed deployment costs, which KPI would justify renewal and what budget line could pay for the system.

## Commercial stop conditions

Do not scale engineering or claim product-market fit when the buyer is not identified, the problem is not budgeted, no site can provide a named owner, no measurable baseline exists, or the pilot has no renewal/expansion decision. Record negative interviews and objections rather than counting interest without commitment.
